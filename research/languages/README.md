# Language-layer evidence policy

The multilingual rules separate three kinds of material:

1. **Normative language references** describe accepted orthography, grammar, treatment, or public-document practice.
2. **Observed author/venue evidence** controls voice, register, and locale in the actual task.
3. **Sepia editorial heuristics** identify patterns worth reviewing; they are not authorship classifiers and are not presented as research findings unless a cited study tested them.

Normative sources do not define a single “human” style. They are used only to avoid incorrect corrections and unwanted locale normalization. The writer's explicit choices and the venue outrank generic style preferences.
